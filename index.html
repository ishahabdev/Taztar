About Teztar Services

Technology-driven commerce built for Pakistan

Who We Are

Pakistan's Most Trusted Digital Commerce Ecosystem

Teztar Services is a technology-driven e-commerce, delivery, and mobility company built to serve people, businesses, and communities through reliability, transparency, and innovation.

We manage storefront creation, product verification, marketing, secure payments, order fulfillment, and last-mile delivery — so merchants can focus entirely on their products.

Our platform also enables overseas Pakistanis to purchase medicines, groceries, and essentials for delivery to families across Pakistan.

To build Pakistan's most trusted, fraud-free, and inclusive digital commerce ecosystem.
